Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

CLEANTEE respects your privacy and follows the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173). This page explains what we collect, why, and your rights.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number and an encrypted (hashed) password.
  • Booking details: service, schedule, address, optional map link and notes.
  • Payment details: payment method, reference number and, if you upload one, a screenshot of your down payment. We never ask for card or online banking passwords.
  • Reviews you choose to submit.

Why we use it

  • To confirm, schedule and perform your cleaning service.
  • To verify your down payment.
  • To send booking updates by email.
  • To keep the website secure and prevent abuse.

Who can see it

Only CLEANTEE administrators and the cleaning staff assigned to your booking (name, address, contact number, schedule and notes for that job only). We do not sell your information. We share it only when required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep booking and payment records as long as needed for service, accounting and legal requirements. You may ask us to delete your account and personal data once these are no longer required.

How we protect it

Passwords are hashed, the site is served over HTTPS, payment screenshots are stored privately, and administrator actions are logged.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw your consent, at any time. You may also complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Contact us

Call or Viber +63 998 577 5917 for any privacy request.
