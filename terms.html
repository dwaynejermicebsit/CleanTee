1. Bookings

A booking request is confirmed only after our team replies with your slot. Each service has a minimum number of hours, shown on the Services page. Please give an accurate address, contact number and access details.

2. Rates and payment

  • Rates are in Philippine pesos. Hourly services are charged per hour as listed; fogging, grease trap and aircon services are charged per request or unit as shown.

  • The online estimate is a guide. The final amount depends on actual hours worked, the size and condition of the space and any extras agreed on site.

3. Rescheduling and cancellation

Bookings are not cancelled, except for emergencies, including inclement weather. Instead, your booking will be rescheduled to a new date and time. Please tell us at least 24 hours before your slot if you need to move it. For an emergency or bad weather, send an emergency cancellation request from My History; our team reviews it and replies by email. No-access visits or late changes may be charged the transport fee.

5. Issues, damage and missing items

If something is not right, use the Report page within 48 hours of the service, with your booking reference and photos if possible. We review every report and contact you. Claims are assessed case by case.
