Our Mission

At CLEANTEE Cleaning Service, our mission is to create cleaner, healthier, and more comfortable spaces for every home and business in Metro Manila. We are committed to delivering reliable, high-quality cleaning solutions through trained professionals, eco-friendly practices, and customer-centered service.

🔭

Our Vision

To be the most sought-after cleaning company in Metro Manila, recognized for our commitment to quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction. We aim to set new industry standards by continuously improving our processes, embracing innovation, and fostering a culture of excellence. Our dedicated team is driven by a passion for cleanliness and a mission to make every space we service healthier and more enjoyable for all.

🌿

Eco-minded

Safe, effective products and careful methods for families, pets and workplaces.

We strive to

  • Elevate the standard of cleanliness by using safe, effective, and industry-approved cleaning methods.
  • Build trust and long-term relationships with clients through transparency, consistency, and respect for their time and space.
  • Improve the well-being of communities by promoting healthy, hygienic environments that enhance productivity and peace of mind.
  • Empower our team through skills training, fair opportunities, and a culture of integrity and excellence.

With every space we clean, we aim to bring comfort, confidence, and cleanliness you can truly see and feel.
